Ways to build. Start with a flat shape (one cuttable piece — coaster, plaque, frame) and flip to 3D build when ready. Ribs join inside (hidden spine, layers slide down) or outside (exoskeleton fins that slide in radially and bite min width into the silhouette); either can be curved or straight. Slots are thickness − kerf for a friction fit, and min width keeps every land and wall thick enough not to snap. Twist, skew, bend and chaos rearrange the stack so they switch to glue-stack automatically. The smoother rounds spiky forms into clean curves — joints follow it exactly. Always burn a kerf test comb first.
