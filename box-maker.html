Material & joint

Joint size is the target finger length. Finger counts are snapped odd so every corner lands on a tab. Kerf widens tabs and narrows slots for a press fit.

Compartments

Widths are relative — 2 1 1 makes the first column twice the others. Add or remove numbers to change the count.
