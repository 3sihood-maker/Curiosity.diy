Box

Millimetres, measured over the outside faces.

Lid

The overlay lid is a shallow lidless box that drops over the top. Clearance is the gap per side between the box wall and the lid rim.

Material & joint

Joint size is the target finger length. Finger counts are snapped odd so every corner lands on a tab. Kerf widens tabs and narrows slots for a press fit.

Compartments

Even splits the cavity equally.

Crossing dividers are always cross-lapped. Free-floating makes a removable egg-crate with no wall or base slots.

Advanced layout

Tap a compartment, then split it. Splits nest, so you can give one region three narrow slots while its neighbour stays a single wide tray. Walls that dead-end into other walls are tabbed through them; nothing cross-laps.

Tap a compartment to select it.
