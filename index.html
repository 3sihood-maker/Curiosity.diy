A home workshop · Free maker tools

Welcome to a workshop of free making tools that help you harness your creativity and design cool things.

Everything on the bench is free to use — no sign-up, no watermark, no catch. If a tool earns its keep, you can shout me a coffee.

Workshop notes

A few things worth knowing before you start pulling tools off the wall.

Free, properly free

No account, no trial, no export limit. Open a tool, make the thing, take the file home.

Runs in your browser

Nothing is uploaded anywhere. Your designs stay on your machine, where they belong.

Made for the local DIY-er

Built on a real bench, for hobby cutters, makerspaces, classrooms and kitchen-table projects.

If a tool saved you a Saturday…

The tools stay free. Coffee keeps the workshop lights on and the next one coming.
