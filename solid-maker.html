Print-honest by design. There's no bend or lean here — both tip the walls past the point a printer can bridge them. Twist, flutes and waves all keep the walls climbing.

Wall thickness is nominal. Set your slicer's wall count from the number here — for a 0.4 nozzle, 2.4 mm is six perimeters. Vases print beautifully in spiral/vase mode: set the wall to one perimeter and let the slicer do the hollowing.
