Shrink or grow the whole mechanism — gears, arms, pins, converters — while dowels, bearings, thickness & paper stay standard. Tooth counts (and so the drawing) are unchanged; the pattern just scales with the machine. These multiply the current size.
—
Select a gear, then mesh a new one beside it (same layer) or stack one on its shaft (next layer up — a compound gear). One crank drives the whole graph; ratios multiply through the stack.
Meshed gears sit on the same plane; stacked gears climb to a new layer on the same shaft. Cut each layer as its own sheet and thread them on dowels with spacers.
