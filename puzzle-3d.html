The Curious 3D Puzzle Maker

Jigsaws you can print.

Tell it your printer bed and how thick you want the pieces, and it lays out a full set of interlocking puzzle pieces — spaced apart, ready to slice — with a built-in fit clearance so they click together straight off the plate. Exports STL, OBJ and SVG.
